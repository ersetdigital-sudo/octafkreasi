Business Flow

Alur Pesanan

  • Customer booking destinasi → status Pending
  • Customer transfer pembayaran
  • Admin klik "Tandai Lunas" → Paid → WhatsApp otomatis
  • Admin konfirmasi → Confirmed
  • Admin kirim e-tiket via WhatsApp
  • Hari H: Scan QR → Check-in
  • Trip selesai → Completed
  • Customer tulis ulasan → Admin approve → tampil publik
